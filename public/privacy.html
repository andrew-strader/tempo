Tempo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

Tempo ("we", "our", or "us") is a scheduling and communication app designed for bands and musicians. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you use Tempo, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile photo when you sign in with Google
  • Phone Number: If you choose to provide it for SMS notifications
  • Band Information: Band names, member lists, rehearsal schedules, and gig details you create
  • Communications: Messages you send through the app's text blast feature
  • Calendar Data: If you connect your Google Calendar, we access your free/busy times (not event details) to help schedule rehearsals

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Tempo service
  • Send you notifications about rehearsals, gigs, and band communications
  • Send SMS messages when you or your band members use the text blast feature
  • Help bands coordinate schedules using calendar availability

SMS Communications

Tempo uses SMS to notify band members about rehearsals, gigs, RSVPs, and group messages. Users opt in affirmatively by checking an unchecked-by-default consent checkbox, either on our public webform at tempocal.app/sms-signup or within their Tempo profile settings. Providing a phone number is entirely optional — the app works fully without it.

When you opt in:

  • You may receive SMS notifications about rehearsals, gigs, RSVPs, and band messages
  • Program Name: Tempo Band Notifications
  • Message Frequency: Varies based on band activity
  • Message and Data Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply
  • Opt-Out: Reply STOP to any message, or remove your phone number from your profile
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance
  • Support: support@tempocal.app

Mobile information and opt-in consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS messages are delivered through Twilio, our messaging service provider. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

For full details on SMS opt-in and opt-out procedures, see our SMS Terms & Consent page.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information:

  • With other members of bands you join (your name, email, and phone if provided)
  • With service providers who help us operate Tempo (such as Twilio for SMS)
  • If required by law or to protect our rights

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and at rest. Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase infrastructure.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access and update your profile information in the app
  • Delete your account by contacting us
  • Opt out of SMS communications by replying STOP
  • Disconnect your Google Calendar at any time

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

Email: support@tempocal.app
